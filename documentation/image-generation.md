# Image generation briefs

The built-in `image_gen` tool generated seven original bitmap assets. Outputs were copied from the tool’s generated-images directory into the project, resized, and encoded as WebP with ImageMagick. No CLI/API fallback was used. All final files are in `assets/images/`.

## home-one-hero.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium academic website hero photograph, landscape 3:2. Two young adult Indian university students, a woman wearing a warm cream shirt and a man in muted blue, thoughtfully preparing competitive entrance exams together at a clean oak desk in a beautiful sunlit modern library. Open notebooks, a laptop, books, subtle green plants. Candid editorial photography, warm natural light, realistic skin and hands, restrained navy cream palette, sophisticated calm optimistic atmosphere. Medium wide shot, students on right and center, no logos, no typography, no watermarks.
