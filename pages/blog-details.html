PREPARE WITH PURPOSE

Make the plan fit the life you have.

A useful study plan begins with an honest look at your week. Before counting chapters, notice when you have time, energy, and a quiet place to work.

01. Choose one clear priority

Review the syllabus and take a short baseline test. Pick one topic you can improve this week. Write the outcome in practical terms: “I can solve basic percentage questions without notes.”

02. Give practice a place

Alternate learning with recall. After a lesson, close your notes and try a small question set. Record the reason for each mistake: a concept gap, a calculation, or a missed instruction.

03. Make revision part of the plan

Leave one session for reviewing earlier work. Revisit the questions you found difficult and explain the solution aloud. Understanding the reasoning is more useful than recognizing the answer.

04. Review the routine itself

At the end of the week, notice what you completed and what got in the way. Reduce an unrealistic target, protect a useful study slot, and choose the next small priority. A sustainable plan can change with you.

A good week of preparation gives you something specific to understand, practice, and revisit.
