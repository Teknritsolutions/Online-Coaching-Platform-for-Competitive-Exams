BANKING FUNDAMENTALS
Original Aurevia sample learning notes

RBI stands for Reserve Bank of India.
A central bank performs functions distinct from a commercial bank.
Simple interest = principal x annual rate x time / 100.

Example: Simple interest on 1,000 at 5% for 2 years is 100.
