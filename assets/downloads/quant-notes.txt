PERCENTAGE ESSENTIALS
Original Aurevia sample learning notes

A percentage is a fraction out of 100.
20% = 20/100 = 1/5.
To find p% of x, multiply x by p/100.
Example: 15% of 200 = 30.

Percentage change = (new - original) / original x 100.
A value increasing from 80 to 100 rises by 25%.

Practice: A number rises by 20% to become 240.
Answer: 240 / 1.2 = 200.
