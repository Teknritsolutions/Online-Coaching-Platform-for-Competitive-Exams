GRAMMAR REVISION CHECKLIST
Original Aurevia sample learning notes

1. Identify the subject before choosing the verb.
2. Check tense consistency.
3. Read the entire sentence for meaning.
4. Check singular and plural agreement.

Example: She has finished the test.
The singular subject she takes has, not have.

Vocabulary: diligent = hard-working and careful.
Practice: Write a sentence that uses diligent in context.
