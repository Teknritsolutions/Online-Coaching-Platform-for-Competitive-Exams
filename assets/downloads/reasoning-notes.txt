LOGICAL THINKING
Original Aurevia sample learning notes

For a number series, check differences, ratios, and alternating rules.
Example: 3, 7, 15, 31 -> multiply by 2 and add 1 -> 63.

For direction questions, sketch the route and label distances.
A route 4 km north and 3 km east has a displacement of 5 km.

Do not assume the reverse of a statement.
All roses are flowers does not mean all flowers are roses.
