FOUNDATION SAMPLE PAPER
Original educational demonstration, not an official previous-year paper.
Suggested time: 5 minutes. Scoring: +2 correct, -0.5 incorrect, 0 unanswered.

1. What is 25% of 160?
A) 20 B) 30 C) 40 D) 60

2. Find the next term: 2, 4, 8, 16, ...
A) 18 B) 24 C) 30 D) 32

3. Choose the correct sentence.
A) They is ready. B) They are ready. C) They am ready. D) They be ready.

ANSWER KEY
1. C. A quarter of 160 is 40.
2. D. Each term doubles.
3. B. The plural subject they takes are.
